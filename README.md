\# Material Manager for Blender



A powerful, intuitive Material Manager for Blender, inspired by Cinema 4D. This add-on provides a centralized panel in the 3D Viewport sidebar to effortlessly select, assign, and manage your scene materials.



\## Features

\* \*\*Select Assigned\*\*: Quickly highlight all mesh objects sharing a specific material. 

\* \*\*Deep Cleanup\*\*: Effectively purge unused materials and deeply disconnected image textures from your .blend file, bypassing Blender's internal fake-user and orphan data limitations.

\* \*\*Workflow Optimized\*\*: Perfect for managing complex shaders, tiled texture maps, normal maps, or keeping your project files clean when setting up cyber-style scenes and product images.



\## Installation

1\. Download the latest .zip release from the right sidebar.

2\. In Blender, go to `Edit` > `Preferences` > `Add-ons`.

3\. Click `Install...` and select the downloaded `.zip` file.

4\. Check the box to enable the add-on. 

5\. Press `N` in the 3D Viewport and locate the \*\*MatManager\*\* tab.
